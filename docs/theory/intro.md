# Introduction

This section describes the mathematical models and numerical formulations implemented in SafeInCave. It covers the thermodynamic model for the fluid contained in the caverns, and the governing equations for the surrounding rocks. This includes the momentum balance equation and the heat diffusion equation. The constitutive models available in SafeInCave are also presented here. Finally, the finite element formulations are also describe for both governing equations.
